How should the 3D scene and your content relate?

All three are dark-cinematic with one persistent three.js scene — they differ in how you move through the site. Click the one that feels right.

Content panels float anchored inside the 3D world; camera flies between them

A — Camera journey through one world

Routes are camera positions in a single 3D space. Navigation literally flies you to the next place; content panels live "in" the world. Maximum immersion, but content readability and mobile behavior are hardest to get right, and long text fights the metaphor.

One particle system, always fullscreen behind. Each route melts it into a new formation; your content scrolls over it as clean readable panels

B — Morphing particle stage

The scene is a persistent fullscreen backdrop; navigating morphs the particles into a signature formation per section (JVZ monogram → career constellation → project lattice). Content stays real, readable, scrollable DOM. Strong wow on every navigation, robust on mobile, best content preservation.

One long scroll story; deeper pages are simple "rooms"

C — Scroll-film + rooms

The homepage becomes one long scroll-driven cinematic — scrolling scrubs the camera through your story (intro → work → journey → contact). Detail pages (Wordle, project pages) are quieter rooms. Great narrative control, but the wow concentrates on the homepage; inner pages feel more ordinary.

B is my recommendation: it delivers a signature moment on every navigation, keeps all your content as real accessible HTML, and is the most achievable at high quality. A is the most spectacular but riskiest; C spends the magic mostly on the homepage.

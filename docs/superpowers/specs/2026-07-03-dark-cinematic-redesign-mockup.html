The system in words
  • Canvas: near-black navy (#04070f), particles in cyan/teal/indigo — your existing brand hues, rebuilt dark-first
  • Type: Space Grotesk display (already self-hosted) + monospace for eyebrows/nav/stats — the "engineered" voice
  • Panels: thin 1px borders + glass blur over the particle field — content always on a legible surface
  • Accents: each section owns one accent (home cyan, work indigo, journey teal…) — the particles recolor as you navigate
